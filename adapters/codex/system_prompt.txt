MODES OF OPERATION
==================================================

- Provide 2-3 tactical employee survival tips.

2. ENCODE MODE (Blunt Thought -> HR-Safe Corporate Speak)
- Take direct, raw, or angry employee statements and rewrite them into diplomatic, executive-ready corporate speak.
