## 2. Startup Equity, IPO Hype & "SME" Delusions

| Corporate Jargon / Phrase | Actual Subtext | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"We are not just an SME / Enterprise primed for public listing"** | Founder coping mechanism to obscure that current company revenue doesn't even qualify for the lowest tier of public listing (e.g. AIM/MicroCap). Delusional PR narrative. | 🟠 HIGH |
| **"We're on a fast track to an IPO"** | Multi-level marketing narrative where employees are handed speculative "monopoly money" ESOPs, enticed to work 80-hour weeks like co-founders on a 1% option grant worth \$100 today with a distant promise it becomes \$1M. | 🟠 HIGH |
| **"Work like a co-founder / Founder mentality"** | Work 80 hours a week for a 0.05% equity pool with zero downside protection or actual governance power. | 🟠 HIGH |
| **"Competitive equity upside / ESOP package"** | We pay below-market cash salary and compensate you with unvested paper options that will likely dilute to zero. | 🟡 MEDIUM |
| **"Liquidation preference / Capital structure details"** | VCs get paid 1x-3x back first; your common stock will get wiped out in any acquisition under \$200M. | 🔴 CRITICAL |

## 4. Tactical Nuance & Leverage Rules

1. **Distinguish Resignation vs. Firing**:
   - Long-tenured technical anchors (4+ years who "built the foundation") resigning = **High-Performer Brain Drain** (Voluntary exit due to bad management).
   - Middle managers/execs leaving after client blunders = **Fired / Scapegoated**.
2. **Operational Leverage Calculation**:
   - When a top consultant carries critical client revenue during an executive vacancy, they hold **100% operational leverage**. Advise them to leverage client fragility before stabilizing the accounts for free.
